Stadium Signal Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Stadium Signal helps users discover sports giveaways, save games, find nearby events, receive giveaway notifications, and export selected games to a calendar. This policy explains the information processed when you use the Stadium Signal iOS app.

Information We Collect

App usage and approximate location

Stadium Signal uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and prioritize giveaway coverage. Analytics may collect app opens, sessions, screens viewed, and interactions such as viewing or saving a giveaway or selecting tickets or directions. These events may include the giveaway's team, league, type, city, and state.

Google Analytics assigns a pseudonymous identifier to an installation of the app and derives approximate geographic information, such as city or region, from network information. Stadium Signal does not send the device's precise Core Location coordinates to Firebase Analytics. Personalized-advertising signals are disabled, and this information is used for measurement and product improvement rather than targeted advertising.

Subscription information

Stadium Signal uses RevenueCat to manage Pro subscriptions. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier, App Store receipt and transaction information, product identifiers, subscription status, and related technical information needed to validate purchases, restore subscriptions, prevent fraud, provide entitlements, and produce subscription analytics. Stadium Signal does not receive payment-card information. Payments are processed by Apple.

Push-notification token

If notifications are enabled, the app sends its Apple Push Notification service token and platform type to the Stadium Signal backend. This token is used only to address giveaway-update notifications to that app installation. It is not an advertising identifier.

Network and device information

When the app contacts Stadium Signal or third-party services, those services may process ordinary request information such as IP address, app and operating-system version, device type, request time, and response status for delivery, security, abuse prevention, and reliability.

Information Processed Only on Your Device

Precise location

If you allow location access, the app uses your current coordinate in memory to find nearby giveaways and determine the nearest free-tier team. Stadium Signal does not upload or retain that precise coordinate. City or ZIP searches use Apple's geocoding services and are subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Saved games and My List notes

Saved games, attendance status, trade notes, names entered into those notes, buying notes, and related My List information are stored locally on your device with iOS file protection. Stadium Signal does not upload this information.

Calendar information

If you choose to export games, the app requests Calendar access so you can select a writable calendar, check for duplicate exported events, and add the games you selected. Stadium Signal does not upload your calendar names, existing events, or exported events. Calendar information may sync through services configured on your device, such as iCloud, under those providers' terms.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide schedules, subscriptions, notifications, maps, directions, and other requested app features;
  • understand which regions, teams, leagues, and giveaway markets need additional coverage;
  • measure feature usage and improve the app;
  • validate purchases and restore subscription access;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the app and its backend services; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We do not use app data to track users across other companies' apps or websites for targeted advertising.

Information is processed by service providers only as needed to operate the app, including:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase for usage analytics and approximate geographic reporting;
  • RevenueCat for subscription management and purchase analytics;
  • Railway and other infrastructure providers used to operate the Stadium Signal API and notification service;
  • Google-hosted published schedule sources;
  • Apple services including the App Store, StoreKit, APNs, Core Location, MapKit, EventKit, and Apple Maps; and
  • Google Maps or Waze when you explicitly choose to open directions with one of those services.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

For information about how Google processes data from apps that use its services, visit Google's partner sites policy.

Data Retention

Local My List data remains on the device until you remove it, clear the list, or uninstall the app. Calendar events remain until removed from the selected calendar. Analytics, subscription, server-log, and notification-token information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to provider settings, legal requirements, security needs, and backup cycles. Invalid or unneeded push tokens may be removed or replaced as devices and installations change.

Your Choices

You may:

  • deny or revoke Location, Calendar, or Notification permission in iOS Settings;
  • remove saved games and local notes within the app;
  • remove exported events in your Calendar app;
  • manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple account;
  • reset or reinstall the app to replace locally generated installation identifiers, subject to provider behavior; and
  • contact us to ask a privacy question or request deletion of information that Stadium Signal can reasonably associate with your app installation.

Some features may not work without the permission or information needed to provide them.

Children

Stadium Signal is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above indicates when the policy was last revised. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate.
